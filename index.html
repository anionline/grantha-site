Sanskritग्रन्थ

A knot. A tying together.

The string that bound palm-leaf manuscripts into a book, so that every leaf stayed in its place.

  1. 01 A bound text.

    A book, a treatise, a scripture. Inscribed palm leaves held together by a thread.

  2. 02 A script.

    A South Indian hand used to write Sanskrit for well over a thousand years.

  3. 03 A measure.

    One grantha is one śloka: thirty-two syllables, a single complete thought.
